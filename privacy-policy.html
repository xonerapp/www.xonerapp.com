Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Xoner (“Xoner”, “we”, “us” or “the App”) collects, uses and handles information when you create an account or use the service.

Xoner is operated by Xoner App. For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, you may contact us at support@xonerapp.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Xoner, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, phone number if provided, and account authentication details.
  • Information received when you choose to sign in using supported third-party authentication services such as Google or Apple.
  • Profile information such as age, gender, ethnicity, country, city, interests, preferences, biography and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Profile photos that you choose to upload.
  • Optional voice introductions that you choose to record or upload as part of your profile.
  • Messages and other communications exchanged with users through the App.
  • Interaction information such as likes, passes, matches, profile interactions and other actions you perform within Xoner.
  • Safety information such as reports, blocks or other actions used to protect users and enforce Xoner's rules.
  • Device and technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure and maintain the App.
  • Push notification tokens where you permit Xoner to send notifications to your device.

3. Human Verification

Xoner requires users to complete a human verification process before accessing the core features of the App.

A selfie image may be used during this process to determine whether a real person is completing the verification.

Xoner does not retain the verification selfie after the verification process has been completed.

We may retain the result of the verification process, such as whether the account successfully completed human verification, so that the user does not need to repeat the process unnecessarily.

Human verification is intended to reduce fake and automated accounts. It is not government-issued identity verification, a background check or a guarantee of a user's identity or behaviour.

4. Location Information

Xoner does not request, collect or track your device's precise GPS location.

Country and city information displayed or used within Xoner is based on information you provide as part of your profile or preferences.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through Xoner to:

  • Create, authenticate and manage your account.
  • Create and display your dating profile.
  • Allow users to discover and interact with other profiles.
  • Support likes, matches, messaging and other core App features.
  • Perform and record completion of human verification.
  • Provide push notifications where you have allowed them.
  • Respond to reports, enforce our rules and help protect users.
  • Detect, investigate and prevent misuse, fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve Xoner's functionality and performance.
  • Respond to support requests and communications.

6. Profile Visibility

Information you add to your Xoner profile may be visible to other users as part of the normal operation of the dating service.

This may include your profile name, age, photos, ethnicity, country, city, interests, biography, preferences, optional voice introduction and other profile information you choose to provide.

Information that is intended only for account administration, authentication or security is not displayed publicly as part of your dating profile.

7. Sharing of Information

Xoner does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to operate the service, including:

  • With other Xoner users where information forms part of your profile or user interactions.
  • With service providers that support functions such as hosting, authentication, media storage, communications or technical infrastructure.
  • Where required to comply with applicable law, legal proceedings or lawful requests from authorities.
  • Where reasonably necessary to investigate misuse, protect users or protect the security and integrity of Xoner.

8. Messages & Communications

Messages exchanged through Xoner may be stored so that users can view their conversation history and so that Xoner can operate messaging, investigate reports, enforce safety rules and comply with legal obligations.

Access to message content is limited to circumstances where it is reasonably necessary for operation of the service, investigation of a report, enforcement of our rules, user safety or legal compliance.

9. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Xoner may store a device notification token so that the App can send alerts relating to activity such as likes, matches, messages or other relevant account events.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

10. Data Retention

Xoner retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal obligations.

  • Account and profile information may be retained while your account remains active.
  • Profile photos and optional voice introductions may be retained while they remain associated with your account.
  • Messages and interaction records may be retained as required to provide App functionality and maintain safety and integrity.
  • Verification selfies are not retained after the verification process has been completed.
  • A record that human verification was successfully completed may be retained with your account.
  • Certain records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

11. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Xoner account through the account deletion feature available within the App.

When an account is deleted, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable legal and operational requirements.

Some limited information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or enforcement purposes.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials.

13. Children's Privacy

Xoner is strictly intended for users aged 18 and above. People under the age of 18 are not permitted to create or use a Xoner account.

14. International Data Processing

Xoner and the service providers used to operate the App may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

15. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other controls provided by applicable law.

You may also withdraw permissions granted through your device, such as notification permissions, using your device settings.

Privacy requests may be submitted to support@xonerapp.com.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Xoner, our practices or applicable legal requirements.

Where appropriate, material changes may be communicated through the App or another reasonable method.

17. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, contact Xoner at support@xonerapp.com.
