Delete Your Xoner Account

Xoner allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data directly from within the Xoner app.

How to Delete Your Account

While signed in to Xoner, open Settings and select Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

What Happens After You Request Deletion

  • Your Xoner account is deactivated immediately.
  • Your profile is no longer available for normal use of the service.
  • Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days.
  • During this 30-day period, you may restore your account by signing back in to Xoner.
  • If you do not restore the account during the 30-day period, the account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
Changed your mind?

Sign back in to Xoner within 30 days of requesting deletion to restore your account.

If You Cannot Access the App

If you cannot access your Xoner account or the in-app deletion feature, contact support@xonerapp.com from the email address associated with your Xoner account.

We may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing an account-related request.

Data Retention

Some limited information may be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, enforcement or other legitimate legal obligations.

For more information about how Xoner handles personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.
