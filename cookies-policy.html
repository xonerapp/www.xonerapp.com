Cookies & Storage Policy

1. Cookies, Browser Storage & App Storage

Cookies are small text files commonly used by websites and web browsers. The Xoner website may use cookies, local browser storage or similar technologies where reasonably necessary for website functionality.

The native Xoner mobile application does not rely on traditional browser cookies in the same way as a website. The App may instead use local device storage, secure storage and similar technologies to provide account, session, preference and security functionality.

2. How Xoner Uses These Technologies

Xoner may use cookies, local storage, secure storage and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in and maintain your session.
  • Remember basic settings and preferences.
  • Support authentication and account security.
  • Maintain and improve app and website performance.
  • Help detect or prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorized activity.

3. Technologies Used

Depending on whether you are using the Xoner website or mobile application, technologies may include:

  • Essential website cookies or browser storage used to provide basic website functionality.
  • Local device storage used by the Xoner mobile application.
  • Secure device storage used where appropriate for authentication or security-related information.
  • Technical or diagnostic technologies used to maintain reliability, security and performance.

4. Third-Party Services

Xoner may use trusted third-party service providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, media storage, communications, notifications and technical infrastructure.

These providers may use their own cookies, device storage or similar technologies where necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy and security practices.

5. Your Choices

When using the Xoner website, you may control or clear browser cookies and local browser storage through your browser settings.

When using the Xoner mobile application, you may manage certain permissions and application data through your device settings.

Clearing essential browser or App storage may sign you out, reset preferences or affect certain functionality.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies & Storage Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Xoner, the technologies we use or applicable legal requirements.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Policy, please contact support@xonerapp.com.
