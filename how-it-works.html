HOW XONER WORKS

A safer way to discover and connect.

1. Create your profile

Start by creating your Xoner profile with your basic details, preferences, photos, and optional profile media.

2. Complete Human Verification

Human Verification is required before users can connect on Xoner, helping reduce fake accounts and create a more authentic community.

3. Discover people

Discover verified users based on your preferences, location choices, age range, and the profile information people choose to share.

4. Connect and chat

When two people connect, they can start a conversation and get to know each other inside Xoner.

5. Stay in control

Xoner includes safety tools that allow users to block or report other users when necessary, helping support a respectful community.
